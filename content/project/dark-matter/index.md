Dark matter is not necessarily smooth. Its small-scale structure can preserve information about particle production, interactions, and the thermal history of the Universe.

I study how miniclusters, streams, solitons, and other compact structures form and evolve, how they interact with stars and black holes, and how they alter direct-detection, radio, lensing, and gravitational signals. This programme combines analytic theory, numerical modelling, and detector-facing phenomenology.

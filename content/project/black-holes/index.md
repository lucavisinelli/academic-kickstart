Black holes provide extreme tests of particle physics and gravity. I study primordial and astrophysical black holes as sources, detectors, and gravitational laboratories for physics beyond the Standard Model.

My work includes evaporation and high-energy messengers from ultralight black holes, superradiant interactions with bosonic fields, compact-object environments, black-hole shadows, and gravitational-wave signatures. These systems connect early-Universe scenarios to observable phenomena across many frequency bands.
